07 — Next quarter

What we do with this.

Six moves that follow directly from the numbers above — designed to protect what worked and fix what didn't.

1

Decode the April spike

April drove roughly 70% of the quarter's GMV. Pin down what powered it — a launch, a seeding wave, or a breakout video — and whether it can be repeated. It's the single biggest lever for Q3.

2

Lean into breadth

3,300+ creators posted and no single creator dominates — the top two are just 18% of GMV. That distributed base is a strength; keep the funnel wide and volume high.

3

Convert the sampled majority

Far more creators received product than converted to sales. A focused re-engagement push to sampled-but-silent creators is low-cost upside.

4

Lead briefs with the proven SKUs

Original Zinc and the Dry Mouth line drove the most GMV. Make them the default hook for new creators; layer in secondary rinses once they're landing sales.

5

Recruit Spanish creators

Only 22 of 447 converting videos were Spanish. The segment is thin and high-upside — a targeted Spanish recruitment push is low-cost, high-ceiling.

6

Reconcile to Seller Center

These figures are video-attributed from Euka. Pull the Seller Center creator and video lists to fold in LIVE and product-card sales and confirm official totals.
